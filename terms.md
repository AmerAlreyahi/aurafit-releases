You use AuraFit at your own risk, and we aren't liable for harm that comes from relying on it, as far as the law allows.

## Your account

You sign in with Google. Keep your account to yourself, and tell us if you think someone else has used it. You can delete your account and its server data any time in Settings › Account.

## AI features

AI estimates and tips are produced by AI models and may be wrong; check them before you save. Each plan has a number of AI credits a month, and each action uses some. Credits start over every month and don't carry over. We may change the AI models we use.

## AuraFit Pro

- **1,000 AI credits a month**, instead of 60 on Free.
- **Cloud backup** of your diary, restored when you sign in on a new phone.

The launch price is **$1.99 a month** or **$9.99 a year**, shown in the app before you pay. Prices may change for new subscriptions; the price you subscribed at stays until you cancel.

## Renewals and cancelling

Pro renews automatically each month or year until you cancel. In the Google Play version it is bought and managed in Google Play (Subscriptions). In the version from GitHub it is bought on a checkout page run by Paddle, which sells it for us as merchant of record; manage or cancel it from Settings › Plans › Manage subscription. After you cancel, Pro lasts until the end of the period you paid for, then your account returns to Free. Your diary stays on your phone either way.

## Refunds

Refunds follow the refund policy of the store you paid through: Google Play's for purchases in Google Play, Paddle's for purchases on the web checkout. Ask them for a refund; we can't refund payments they handle. If you're not sure where to ask, email us.
